Project playbook

Five artifacts I run every project on.

Client plans are confidential, so these use made-up data. They show how I track a project, not a client’s work. The client decides what to build and why. My job is to turn that into requirements and a plan, and deliver it.

Delivery roadmap

Milestones and sprints on one view. The bars at the right edge continue into the next release.

Risk and dependency register

Reviewed every Friday. A risk scores probability × impact (each 1 to 5). Anything above 12 goes to the client that day.

Critical path:Requirements→Architecture→Core build→Automation→Testing→UAT

Decision and trade-off log

When scope, time and quality pull against each other, the options go to the client in writing, and the decision is recorded once.

Requirements traceability matrix

Each requirement is traced to design, build and test, so nothing is built without a reason and nothing is left untested.
