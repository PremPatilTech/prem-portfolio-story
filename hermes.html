Hermes Trading OS

I built Hermes to practise delivery discipline on a project where being wrong costs money. Every change starts with a requirement, a risk rule and an acceptance test, and every decision is written down.

  • PRDWhat the system must do, and why.
  • RiskFail-closed rules: when something is unclear, the system does nothing.
  • TestEach rule is checked against random-entry baselines on years of historical data. Failures are reported plainly.
  • GateNo profit claim before 100 live trades.

The repository holds the PRD, architecture, decision records and test and UAT plans.
